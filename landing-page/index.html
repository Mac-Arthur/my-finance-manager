Good questions

Before you bring
the statement.

Does myAvailable connect to my bank?+

No. You import a supported statement yourself. The app does not hold bank credentials, move money or show a live bank balance.

Which currencies work?+

GHS, USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, AUD, NGN, KES and ZAR are supported. Accounts in different currencies stay separate because the app does not invent exchange rates.

Do all statement PDFs work?+

Searchable PDFs with a recognisable transaction table have the best chance. Unusual or scanned layouts may need a CSV export. The app refuses ambiguous rows rather than guessing silently.

Where is my data stored?+

Inside the browser installation where you imported it. Use the encrypted backup feature before clearing site data or changing devices.
