Your statements stay on your device.

This notice explains how myAvailable version {{ version }} handles information. Last updated 15 November 2026.

What the app stores

Imported statement files, extracted transactions, your local profile, preferences, category corrections, budgets and transfer decisions are stored in this browser. They are not synced between devices.

What is processed locally

PDF and CSV reading, categorisation, balance checks, dashboards and encrypted backups run on your device. PDF passwords are used only to open the selected file and are not stored.
